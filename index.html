Good to know before you book.

Can we bring our own caterer, cake or alcohol?

La Mariposa is a licensed facility, so all food comes from our kitchen and all alcohol is served by our bar team. The one exception is dessert: you can bring a cake from a licensed bakery, with a $4 per person fee for outside desserts.

Is there a guest minimum?

Saturday events need at least 100 adult guests. Friday and Sunday events need at least 75. Weekday events have no published minimum.

How does the deposit work?

A 25% non-refundable deposit reserves your date. A second 25% is due three months after booking. Both are applied to your final balance, and your event agreement lists every date and term.

What hours can we use the venue?

Events run between 10 a.m. and 1 a.m. Ceremony and reception packages include 5 hours, reception-only packages include 4, and the Starlight Room opens 4 hours early for setup. Extra time is $500 per hour.

Are there any decor rules?

We don't supply centerpieces, and we're happy to refer a florist. Outdoors, skip rice and artificial petals; bubbles and birdseed are welcome. No glitter or confetti on tables.

Do you host events other than weddings?

Yes. Quinceañeras, bar mitzvahs, anniversaries, memorial receptions, small business meetings and large corporate functions all fit the estate.

How do guests find you?

We're at 1501 N. Houghton Rd., Tucson, AZ 85749. The property can only be reached from N Houghton Rd., so put that on your invitations.
